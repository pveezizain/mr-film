The Chini Saga

FANTASY · ADVENTURE · MYSTERY · TASIK CHINI

Synopsis

Adam, a UNESCO researcher, uncovers maps and keys that lead him to the ancient mystery of Tasik Chini. Suddenly transported two thousand years into the past, he finds himself caught between Florisia, a village threatened by the power of a black dragon, and Kymersia, a hidden kingdom beneath the lake ruled by seven princesses. As an old legend about a magical dragon egg begins to repeat itself, Adam must discover the truth behind the lake’s secrets and help prevent the kingdoms from falling into darkness.

A fictional fantasy adventure set around the world of Tasik Chini. The kingdoms, characters and dragon mythology are part of the film’s story.
